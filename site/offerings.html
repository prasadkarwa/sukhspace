Offerings

Each session is held with care, from hormonal health to pregnancy, recovery, weight management, breath and ritual. Every one can be joined online or in Dhanbad.

Women’s health

For women living with PCOS or PCOD, irregular periods, peri-menopause, or on a fertility journey

Gentle, targeted practice to support cycle health, stress and weight without pushing an already stressed body harder. It is designed to sit alongside your gynaecologist’s care, never to replace it.

Men’s health

Support for fertility planning, stress and cortisol, hormonal and prostate wellbeing

Practice that helps the body step out of constant high alert, so sleep, recovery and vitality have room to return. A good fit when you and your partner are planning a family together.

Pre-natal sessions

Safe, nurturing yoga for each trimester

Pelvic strength, hip mobility, lower-back care, and quiet time to connect with your baby. Breathing practices and mudras keep you calm through the months ahead. Private 1:1 sessions are available for pregnant women.

Slow, structured support to rebuild core strength, ease the body and restore energy as you find your feet in the beautiful, demanding journey of motherhood.

From the first trimester to the fourth

Pinki is a certified pre and postnatal yoga specialist, and pregnant women are the only students offered private 1:1 sessions. Here is how the practice changes as you do.

  1. Through each trimester

    Safe, nurturing yoga matched to the weeks you’re in: pelvic strength, hip mobility and lower-back care.

  2. Breath, mudras and chanting

    Pre-natal breathing, mudras and chanting designed for the wellbeing of mother and child.

  3. Preparing for birth

    Labour visualisation and Yoga Nidra in the third trimester, so you meet birth calm and prepared.

  4. “The post-natal recovery program helped me regain my physical strength and emotional balance after delivery.”

    Ritu V., Pune

    The fourth trimester

    Postpartum recovery for the stress and pain that often go unheard, rebuilding your strength gently.
