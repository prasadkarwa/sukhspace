खkhaspace

Sukh is, quite literally, good space.

In Sanskrit, sukha joins su, good, with kha, space: the smooth hub of a wheel that turns with ease. Modern life keeps our bodies in a perpetual state of high alert. Every practice here is about making room again, in the breath, in the body and in the day, so you can move beyond physical fitness into deeper rest, strength and calm.

    Ask a question
    Is yoga safe during pregnancy?

    Pre-natal sessions are built for pregnancy: every practice is chosen for the trimester you are in, and Pinki is a certified pre and postnatal yoga specialist. Please check with your doctor before you begin, and tell Pinki about anything your doctor has flagged.

    Can I join online if I don’t live in Dhanbad?

    Yes. Classes run live online, and students practise from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata and beyond. In-person sessions are held in Dhanbad, Jharkhand.

    When are the batches?

    Morning batches run between 8:00 AM and 12:00 PM, three times a week. Evening batches run from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM. Message Pinki and she will suggest the batch that fits your routine.

    Do you offer private 1:1 sessions?

    Private 1:1 sessions are reserved for pregnant women, so the practice can follow your pregnancy week by week. Everyone else joins a small group batch.

    How do I start, and what does it cost?

    Send a WhatsApp message with where you are in your journey. Pinki replies personally with batch timings, fees and the session that suits you. Customised diet plans are an optional add-on from ₹3,500 a month.

    What if it isn’t right for me?

    A refund can be requested after your first live session. After more than one session, fees are non-refundable. The full policy is on the terms page.
